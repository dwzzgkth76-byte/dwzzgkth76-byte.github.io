GraceDose

Medication reminders at the times you set, with Taken and Snooze right on your lock screen, and a simple record of every dose you mark.

For iPhone and iPad. Every reminder feature is free, with no ads and no account. Everything you enter stays on your device.

  • Time-sensitive reminders, with medicine names hidden on the lock screen by default
  • Daily, chosen-weekday, every-few-days, monthly or one-time schedules
  • Your full dose history, with CSV and PDF export
  • Appointment reminders
  • An optional Reflections tab with short Christian prayers and reflections
  • Optional GraceDose Pro: caregiver profiles kept on this device, refill reminders, reports and trends, Home Screen widgets and more reminder sounds

GraceDose is a reminder and tracking app. It doesn't diagnose, prescribe or recommend doses. Always follow your prescription and your care team's instructions.
