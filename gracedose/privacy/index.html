GraceDose Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-19
App: GraceDose: Medication Reminder (iPhone and iPad)
Who we are: GraceDose is made by Peach & Rocket Studio and published on the App Store by Atul Das, trading as Peach & Rocket Studio. Email: atuldasofficial@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your device. Your medicines, reminders, appointments, dose history and notes are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive them.
  • No account, no servers, no sync. GraceDose has no sign-in, sends your information to no server of ours and doesn't sync it through iCloud.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app contains no advertising or ad SDK, no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own, and no tracking. We don't sell or share data.
  • App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your name, email or payment details.

1. What GraceDose stores, and where

GraceDose saves the following only on your device, in the app's own storage, which iOS protects with your device passcode:

  • medicines you add (names, strengths, doses and notes), their schedules and reminder times
  • dose records: the doses you mark Taken or Skipped, and when
  • appointments and their reminders
  • an optional first name and optional care notes
  • caregiver profiles for people you look after, and refill counts, if you use GraceDose Pro (section 3)
  • your private reflection journal, if you use the optional Reflections tab
  • your settings, such as reminder sound and lock-screen privacy

Everything you type is optional and entered by you. GraceDose doesn't ask for your email address, phone number or age. If an earlier version stored any of these on your device, they're removed when you update.

  • Widgets (GraceDose Pro) read a copy of today's reminders from a shared area on the same device. Widget data doesn't leave your device.
  • Device backups: if your iPhone or iPad is backed up with iCloud Backup or to a computer, GraceDose's data is included in your backup, which Apple handles under your Apple settings and Apple's Privacy Policy. We can't access it.
  • No sync. GraceDose doesn't sync your data between devices or store it in iCloud. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that version is released.

2. What we never do

  • We don't collect, receive or store your health information, or any other information about you, from the app.
  • We don't have accounts or servers for the app.
  • We don't show ads, include any advertising SDK or use your data for advertising.
  • We don't use analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your information.

3. Caregiver profiles (GraceDose Pro)

With GraceDose Pro you can add profiles for people you look after, so their medicines and reminders sit alongside yours. These profiles, and everything you enter for them, are stored only on this device. They're not sent to us, to the person you care for, or to anyone else, and they're not synced to their devices. Please only add someone's details if they're happy for you to.

4. Reminders and notifications

Reminders are scheduled and delivered on your device by iOS. No reminder content passes through us.

  • Medicine names are hidden on the lock screen by default. Reminders say "Time for your medicine" and show details only in the app. You can change this in Settings › Hide medicine names on the lock screen.
  • Dose reminders can be Time Sensitive, so they can appear during Focus if you allow it in iOS Settings.
  • If you have many medicines: iOS limits how many reminders an app can schedule ahead (64). If you have many medicines or frequent doses, open GraceDose now and then so upcoming reminders stay scheduled.

5. Export, backup and import

You can export your dose history as a CSV or PDF file, or export a GraceDose backup file. You choose where it goes, for example Files, AirDrop or Mail. Once a file leaves the app, it's handled by wherever you send it and that service's privacy policy. We never receive it. Importing a backup reads the file on your device.

6. Purchases: GraceDose Pro

GraceDose Pro is optional. It's offered as a yearly auto-renewing subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, and both are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple tells the app on your device whether Pro is unlocked (using Apple's StoreKit). We don't receive your name, email address, payment details or Apple Account ID. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

  • Manage or cancel a subscription: in iOS Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, or in GraceDose under Settings › GraceDose Pro › Manage subscription. Apple explains how in Cancel a subscription from Apple. Deleting the app or using Delete all data doesn't cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple explains how in Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple.

7. Information Apple may share with us

Apple gives developers aggregated App Store statistics, such as download counts, and crash reports from people who chose to share them with developers. These don't identify you or include anything you entered in GraceDose. You control this sharing in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

8. If you email us

If you email atuldasofficial@gmail.com, including from Help and support in the app, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply to you, and we never add you to a mailing list. Please don't send medication lists or medical records. Our email is hosted by Gmail (Google), which may store emails on servers outside your country, under the Google Privacy Policy. We delete support emails within 12 months, or sooner if you ask. Under the UK and EU GDPR, we rely on our legitimate interest in answering you (Article 6(1)(f)).

9. Our website

This website (dwzzgkth76-byte.github.io/gracedose) has no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other sites. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to run and secure the service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don't receive or use those logs.

10. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings › Delete all data removes your medicines, history, appointments, details, caregiver profiles and journal from this device, and cancels every scheduled reminder. It can't be undone, so export a backup first if you might need it.
  • Delete the app to remove all its data from that device. Copies may remain in your device backups and in any files you exported, until you delete them.
  • Subscriptions are managed by Apple and must be cancelled separately (section 6).
  • Support emails are deleted within 12 months (section 8).

11. Children

GraceDose is designed for adults managing their own routines or helping someone they care for. It's not directed at children under 13 and isn't in the App Store Kids category. Because the app sends us nothing, we don't collect information from children through it. If you're a parent and believe your child has emailed us, let us know and we'll delete the email.

12. Not medical advice

GraceDose is a reminder and tracking app. It doesn't diagnose, prescribe, recommend or calculate doses, and it doesn't replace professional care. Always follow your prescription and your care team's instructions. Reminders depend on your device's settings, so don't rely on any app alone for time-critical treatment. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services. The optional Reflections tab is encouragement, not treatment.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Because everything you enter stays on your device, it's in your hands in the app (sections 5 and 10). The only personal information we may hold is email you've sent us; email us and we'll help. You can also complain to your data protection authority (for example the UK ICO or the Irish Data Protection Commission).

14. Changes to this policy

If we change how GraceDose handles data, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change ships, and update the app's App Store privacy label to match. For significant changes, we'll also mention them in the App Store release notes.
