GraceDose is a reminder and tracking app, not medical advice. For questions about your medicines or doses, ask your doctor, pharmacist or care team. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Reminders aren't arriving

  1. Go to iOS Settings › Notifications › GraceDose and allow notifications, sounds and Time Sensitive Notifications.
  2. In your Focus settings, allow GraceDose (or time-sensitive notifications) for the Focus modes you use.
  3. In GraceDose, open Settings › Test a reminder in 10 seconds.
  4. If you have many medicines or frequent doses, open GraceDose now and then. iOS lets an app schedule only a limited number of reminders ahead (64), so opening the app tops up the upcoming ones. Settings › Notifications shows how far ahead your reminders are scheduled, and the last scheduled notification asks you to open GraceDose before they run out.
  5. GraceDose can't override your phone's settings. Don't rely on any app alone for time-critical treatment.

"Open GraceDose to keep reminders coming"

iOS lets an app schedule only 64 notifications ahead. With many medicines or frequent doses, GraceDose can reach that limit before all your upcoming reminders are scheduled. When that happens, the last notification it schedules is this message, set for about an hour before the first reminder that isn't scheduled yet. Open GraceDose and it schedules your next reminders. Settings › Notifications shows how far ahead your reminders are scheduled.

Follow-up reminders

If you don't mark a dose, GraceDose reminds you again (by default 2 times, 10 minutes apart). You can change this or turn it off in Settings. Marking Taken or Skipped stops the follow-ups for that dose.

Taken and Snooze from the lock screen

Press and hold a reminder to see Taken and Snooze. Medicine names are hidden on the lock screen by default; to show them, turn off Settings › Hide medicine names on the lock screen.

Logging a late dose

Doses from the evening before can still be marked for 4 hours after midnight, so a late-night dose taken at 12:30 am counts for the right day.

Correcting the last 7 days

Open Settings › Dose history and export › Correct the last 7 days to mark a dose as Taken or Skipped, or to clear a mark. You can correct today and the 6 days before it. Future days can't be marked. A corrected dose keeps the time it was scheduled for that day, even if you've changed the medicine's time since.

Travelling and time zones

Medicine times follow your phone's local time. Appointments stay at their booked time. Check your routine with your care team when you change time zones.

Moving to a new phone

GraceDose has no account and doesn't use iCloud for your data. Use Settings › Export a backup, then Import a backup on the new phone. You can also restore the whole phone from a device backup.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete all data removes everything GraceDose stored on this device and cancels every reminder. It can't be undone, so export a backup first if you might need it. It doesn't cancel a subscription (see below).

If GraceDose says your data couldn't be read

Your data hasn't been deleted. Choose Export the unreadable file, then email it to us if you'd like help, or choose Start fresh.

Reflections

The Reflections tab has optional prayers, reflections and short audio readings, plus a private journal. It's free. To hide it, turn off Settings › Show Reflections tab.

GraceDose Pro

Pro is optional, with no ads in either version. It's available as a yearly auto-renewing subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase; the App Store shows your local price before you buy. Pro adds:

  • caregiver profiles for the people you look after, kept on this device
  • refill countdown and refill reminders
  • reports and trends: summaries by day, by medicine and by person
  • Home Screen widgets
  • more reminder sounds

Always free: reminders, Taken and Snooze, follow-ups, lock-screen privacy, as many medicines as you need, your full dose history with CSV and PDF export, Reflections, and Delete all data. Pro reports are summaries; exporting your raw history is always free.

Restore purchases: on a new device, open Settings › GraceDose Pro › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open iOS Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › GraceDose, or in GraceDose open Settings › GraceDose Pro › Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next renewal; Pro stays active until the period ends. Apple explains this in Cancel a subscription from Apple.

If you buy Lifetime while a yearly subscription is active, cancel the yearly subscription there so it doesn't renew.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple, and only Apple can issue refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com; see Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple.

Privacy

Everything you enter stays on your device. GraceDose has no account, no servers, no sync, no ads and no analytics. Read the Privacy Policy.
