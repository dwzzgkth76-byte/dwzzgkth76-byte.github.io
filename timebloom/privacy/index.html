Timebloom Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

App: Timebloom: Kids Visual Timer (iPhone and iPad)

Who we are: Atul Das, trading as Peach & Rocket Studio. Email: atuldasofficial@gmail.com. We are the only operator of the app; nobody else collects or holds information through it.

The short version

  • We don't collect any personal data. Not from you and not from your child.
  • There are no ads. None, ever.
  • There's no analytics and no third-party code. Timebloom contains no tracking, advertising or analytics software from other companies.
  • Your timers stay on your device. Timers, routines, labels and settings are saved only on the iPhone or iPad you use.
  • Apple handles purchases. If you buy Timebloom Pro, Apple processes the payment. We never see your card or Apple Account details.

What the app stores, and where

Timebloom saves the things you create (timers, routines, labels, icons, theme and sound choices, and your Pro unlock status) only on your device, in the app's own storage. We don't run any servers for the app, and none of this information is sent to us.

  • If your device is backed up with iCloud or a computer, this app data may be included in your backup. Apple handles that under your own Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy. We can't access it.
  • If you delete the app, its data on that device is deleted too.

Notifications and Live Activities

Timebloom uses local notifications and Live Activities so the chime and countdown still work when the app is closed. These are scheduled on your device. We don't use push notifications and we don't send marketing messages.

In-app purchases

Timebloom Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple tells the app on your device whether Pro is unlocked. We don't receive your name, email, payment details or Apple Account ID. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information Apple may share with us

Apple gives developers aggregated App Store statistics (for example, download counts and crash reports from people who chose to share them with developers). These statistics don't identify you, and we only use them to improve the app. You control crash and analytics sharing in your device's Settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

Children's privacy

Timebloom is listed in the App Store Kids category for ages 5 and under. It's made for young children to look at and use with a parent, carer or preschool, nursery or kindy staff nearby, so we built it to collect nothing, from anyone, of any age:

  • No accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for a name, birthday, email, photo, voice or location.
  • No ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics, in line with Apple's App Review Guidelines for apps aimed at children (Guidelines 1.3 and 5.1.4).
  • A grown-up check (parental gate) sits in front of purchases, Restore Purchases, links out of the app, sharing, Settings and the permission prompts (such as notifications). Children under 5 are mostly pre-readers, so the check uses a task an adult can do and a young child can't.
  • No nudges. Nothing in the child-facing screens asks a child to buy, share, rate or come back.
  • Nothing can be made public. There's no chat, no sharing with other users and no public profile, so a child can't post or publish personal information through Timebloom.
  • United States (COPPA): Timebloom is directed to children (ages 5 and under), and we don't collect, use or disclose personal information from any child under 13. No persistent identifiers are collected either, so no parental consent is needed. If we ever planned to change that, we would ask for verifiable parental consent first, as COPPA requires.
  • United Kingdom (Age Appropriate Design Code / "Children's Code"): the Code covers services likely to be used by under-18s, and Timebloom is aimed at under-5s. Privacy is high by default. No data is collected, there's no profiling, no geolocation and no nudge techniques, and the app works fully without any data being shared.
  • EU and UK GDPR, including the rules for children's data (Article 8, "GDPR-K"): we don't process children's personal data, so no consent is needed.
  • Other places (for example Australia, New Zealand and Canada): the same practice applies everywhere. We collect nothing.

How long we keep children's information (our retention policy). Timebloom doesn't collect children's personal information, so normally there's nothing to keep. If a support email comes from a child, we reply once and then delete it. We don't use the child's email address or message to contact the child again or for anything else, and we don't share it. The same applies to any other personal information from a child that reaches us.

Parents' choices. A parent or carer can email atuldasofficial@gmail.com at any time to ask what we hold about their child (normally nothing), to have it deleted, or to tell us not to collect or use it. We'll reply and act on the request.

If you contact us

If you email atuldasofficial@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you; under UK and EU GDPR our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your message (Article 6(1)(f)). Our support email is hosted by Gmail (Google), which may store emails on servers outside the UK and the EU. Google's handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. We delete support emails within 12 months, or sooner if you ask. Please don't include a child's personal details.

Where to find this policy

This policy is linked from the App Store listing, from our website, and inside the app in the grown-up Settings area (behind the grown-up check, so a child can't open the link by accident).

Our website

Our website (https://dwzzgkth76-byte.github.io/timebloom/) has no cookies, analytics or trackers. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to run and secure the service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don't receive or use those logs.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data from the app, there's normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you've emailed us, you can ask us to see, correct or delete that email at any time. To remove everything Timebloom stores on your device, delete the app. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (for example the UK ICO or the Irish Data Protection Commission).

Changes to this policy

If we change how Timebloom handles data, we'll update this page and the effective date before the change ships, and update the app's App Store privacy label to match.
