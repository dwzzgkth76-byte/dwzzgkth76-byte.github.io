Quick answers

The chime didn't play when the app was closed.

Check that notifications are on for Timebloom (Settings → Notifications → Timebloom) and that your ring/silent switch and Focus mode allow sounds. Timebloom keeps time from the clock, so the timer stays accurate while your device is locked.

How do I leave the kid view?

Press and hold the gear button for 2 seconds, then complete the grown-up check. To lock the screen to Timebloom completely, you can use iOS Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access).

I bought Pro. How do I get it on another device?

Open Timebloom on the other device, go to Settings (behind the grown-up check) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Can my family share Pro?

Yes. Timebloom Pro supports Family Sharing. If Family Sharing is set up on your Apple Account, you and up to five family members (up to 6 people) can use Pro on your own devices at no extra cost. If Pro doesn't appear on a family member's device, open Timebloom there, go to the grown-up settings and tap Restore purchases.

Is Timebloom Pro a subscription?

No. It's a single one-time purchase. There's no subscription and no renewal.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Timebloom show ads or collect data?

No ads, ever, and no personal data collected. See our privacy policy.

Can my preschool, nursery or kindy buy Timebloom Pro in bulk?

Not yet. Each family or device unlocks Pro separately with a one-time purchase. With Family Sharing, one purchase covers you and up to five family members (up to 6 people in total). Bulk purchasing for preschools, nurseries and kindys isn't available yet. The free timer, labels, the Potty preset and the kid view work on every device without buying anything.
