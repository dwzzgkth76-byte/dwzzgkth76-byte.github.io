Revolt & Rule Support

Revolt & Rule support is by email: atuldasofficial@gmail.com

Please include your device model, iOS version, and the app version shown under Privacy & app information. Do not send a save file unless you are asking about that save, and use an invented character name if the file contains one you care about.

Saves

Progress is stored on the device. There is no account and no iCloud sync. Uninstalling the app deletes the local save. Before you delete the app, use Export and keep the file somewhere you control. Import reads that file back into a slot you choose.

If the app opens on "Your save needs attention," the stored data has not been wiped. Use Export recovery data, then write to the address above with the app version. Closing and reopening the app retries the load.

A life that is already past Act I on a device that does not own Full Career stays on the device. The app does not delete or rewrite that save. You can read it, or restore Full Career with the same Apple ID to keep playing it.

Purchases

Act I, through the first election, is free. Full Career is a one-time purchase (US $8.99 on the price tier used for this product; the App Store shows the price for your country). It unlocks the rest of the career to age 82, with no subscription, no interstitial ads, and future Westmere campaign updates included. Restore it from Menu → Game menu → Full Career, then Restore Purchases, with the same Apple ID. If the store has not made the product available yet, try again when you are online. A cancelled purchase leaves Act I intact. Rewarded extras stay optional, including after you buy Full Career.

Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads

Free play may show an interstitial after a chapter is complete, when an ad is available and your privacy choices allow it. Full Career turns those interstitials off after the store confirms the purchase. Rewarded ads are optional and are not required to finish a chapter. After chapter 1, you can choose whether later ads may be personalized. If you decline, ads stay non-personalized.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open Menu → Game menu → Privacy & app information, then Advertising privacy choices (Google's form, where one applies to you, for example in the EEA, UK or Switzerland). For ad tracking, use iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Privacy
