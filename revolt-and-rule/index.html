Revolt & Rule

A political life simulator. Build a movement, win your first election and live with the choices of a whole career in a fictional republic.

For iPhone and iPad. Act I, through the first election, is free, with ads only after a chapter is complete. Full Career is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the rest of the career to age 82, with no interstitial ads and future Westmere campaign updates included.
