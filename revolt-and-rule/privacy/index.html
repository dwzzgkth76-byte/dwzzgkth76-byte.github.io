Revolt & Rule Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026
App: Revolt & Rule: Politics Game (iPhone and iPad)
Who we are: Atul Das, trading as Peach & Rocket Studio. Address: Building 15, Street 986, Zone 22, Doha, Qatar. Phone: +974 3339 6945. Email: atuldasofficial@gmail.com. We make and run Revolt & Rule, a political life simulator. The seller is Atul Das. Free play shows ads served by Google AdMob, which receives some data as described below.

The short version

  • Your saves stay on your device. There are no player accounts and no studio server that receives your saves or gameplay.
  • Free play shows ads from Google AdMob, only after a chapter is complete, plus optional rewarded ads you choose to watch. To serve and measure those ads, Google receives some device and usage data (section 2).
  • You choose about personalised ads. Ads are personalised only if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt and, where Google's consent form applies, it allows ads. Otherwise the app asks for non-personalised ads (section 3).
  • Full Career is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. It is not a subscription (section 4).

1. What stays on your device

The game has no player accounts and no studio server that receives your saves. Your character, choices, and the three save slots are stored on the device (and in a local recovery copy). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes that save. Your device vendor may include app data in a device backup. Export uses the system share sheet; you choose where a copy goes. Import reads only the file you pick.

The character name is whatever you type. Use an invented name if you prefer. Gameplay is not sent to us.

Apple's MetricKit can record crash and performance diagnostics. The app writes those payloads under Application Support on the device and does not upload them. They can still appear in Xcode Organizer through Apple's own reporting.

2. Advertising

Free play can show a Google AdMob interstitial only after a chapter is complete: when the story enters rounds 4, 9, 13, 19, 24, and 29, and when you leave the ending screen. A short "Chapter complete" card comes first. An interstitial is not shown on a choice, a confirmation, or "Move forward," and it is not shown on the Full Career offer.

Rewarded ads are optional. You choose whether to watch one. A reward is granted only after a completed view. If you own Full Career, the app can grant that extra without showing an ad. Declining a rewarded ad does not change your stats.

Google may still process device, network, and ad-interaction data to deliver an ad, limit fraud, and measure performance. That can include an IP address (which can estimate a coarse location), a device identifier, advertising interactions, diagnostics, and crash or performance data. Google's own privacy manifest travels with the Mobile Ads SDK and is merged into the app's privacy report. See Google's description of AdMob data use, the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We don't receive this data in identifiable form. We only see aggregate AdMob reports, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned. We don't send Google your character, choices or saves.

3. Google consent and tracking (ATT) choices

Google's consent form (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Where Google requires consent, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) when free play starts, before any ad is requested. If the form is still waiting for an answer when you finish chapter 1, it is shown again before the tracking prompt. You can accept, refuse or choose purposes.

To change your consent choices later: open Menu → Game menu → Privacy & app information, then tap Advertising privacy choices. This opens Google's privacy options form where one applies to you. Where no form applies, the app tells you so.

Apple's tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency). The prompt is not shown on first launch. After you finish chapter 1 and tap Continue on that chapter's turning point, the app may ask once, through App Tracking Transparency, whether later ads may be more relevant to you. Where a Google consent form is required, the form is shown before the tracking prompt.

  • If you choose Allow, and the consent form allows ads, the app may request personalised ads.
  • If you choose Ask App Not to Track, or never answer, the app can't read your advertising identifier and asks for non-personalised ads. Google may still use limited data, such as your IP address, to deliver ads, limit fraud and measure performance (section 2).
  • To change this later: use iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, or Settings › Revolt & Rule. Apple explains this in If an app asks to track your activity.

Personalised ads are requested only when you allow tracking and the consent form allows ads. Otherwise the app asks for non-personalised ads. None of these choices changes how the game plays.

4. Full Career

Act I, through the first election, is free. Full Career is a one-time in-app purchase. The price tier used for this product is US $8.99; the App Store shows the price for your country, and the app shows that store price. It is not a subscription. It unlocks the rest of the career to age 82, with no interstitial ads, and future Westmere campaign updates included. Rewarded extras stay optional.

Apple processes the payment. The app keeps a local record of the current store entitlement for that Apple ID. Restore purchases uses the same Apple ID: Menu → Game menu → Full Career, then Restore Purchases. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. What we do not do

  • No account, and no gameplay or save upload to a studio server.
  • No studio analytics SDK, and no upload of MetricKit payloads by the app.
  • No sale of personal information by us.
  • No subscription is offered.
  • The app is rated 16+. It is not directed at children.

6. Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include, such as your device model, iOS version, app version or a save file you choose to attach. We use this only to reply to you, and we never add you to a mailing list. Our email is hosted by Gmail (Google), which may store emails on servers outside your country, under the Google Privacy Policy. We delete support emails within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

7. Data retention

  • Saves and settings stay on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Copies may remain in your device backups and in any files you exported, until you delete them.
  • MetricKit diagnostics stay on your device until you uninstall the app. The app does not upload them.
  • Support emails are deleted within 12 months (section 6).
  • Ad data is held by Google, not by us, under Google's own retention policies. We only see aggregate AdMob reports.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple. The app's local record of your Full Career entitlement is removed when you uninstall the app, and Restore Purchases brings it back.

8. Your rights and choices

  • In the game: export, import or delete your saves, and change your advertising choices (section 3). Because your saves never leave your device, most of your data is in your hands.
  • EEA and UK rights: if you're in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom (and in other places with similar laws), you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (withdrawing doesn't affect processing that happened before). You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority, for example the UK Information Commissioner's Office or the data protection authority where you live in the EEA.
  • What we hold: normally only emails you've sent us. Ad data is held by Google; you can manage some Google ad settings at My Ad Center.
  • How to reach us: email atuldasofficial@gmail.com. We'll help with anything we hold, or point you to the right place.
