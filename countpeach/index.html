Countpeach

A calm tally counter for anything you count, with a big tap area, undo for every mistake and every tap saved the moment you make it.

For iPhone and iPad. Free with small banner ads that never appear while you count. Countpeach Pro is an optional one-time purchase that removes ads and adds interactive Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, unlimited counters, CSV export and more.
