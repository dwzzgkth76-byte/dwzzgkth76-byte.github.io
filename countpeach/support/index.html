Counting

How do I count? Open a counter and tap anywhere on the big coloured area. Each tap adds the counter's step (1 unless you changed it), with a small vibration so you can count without looking.

How do I subtract? Tap the − button, or swipe down on the counter (you can turn swiping off in Settings › Counting).

Can I type a number? Yes. Tap ••• › Set count…, or press and hold the number.

What is "Tasbih / Dhikr"? It's a preset you can pick when you create a counter. It counts in rounds of 33 (or 99), gives a gentle buzz at the end of each round, and keeps your total and the number of rounds completed.

Does my count survive closing the app? Yes. Every tap is saved on your device before the vibration plays, so counts survive force-quits, crashes and app updates.

Undo and reset

I tapped by mistake. How do I undo? Tap Undo at the bottom of the counter. You can undo many steps in a row, including a reset, a typed value and taps made from a widget.

How do I reset a counter? Tap ••• › Reset… and confirm. You can still undo it, and the old count stays in History.

Widgets (Pro)

How do I add a widget? Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, find Countpeach and choose a size. Then touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, and pick a counter.

Can I count from the Lock Screen? Yes, with the circular or rectangular Lock Screen widget. iOS runs the tap after you unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

My widget says "Widgets are part of Pro". Widgets need Countpeach Pro. Open the app to unlock or restore it.

Countpeach Pro, restoring and removing ads

What does Pro include? It removes all ads, and adds unlimited counters (the free version has 3), the grid view, Home and Lock Screen widgets, full history and CSV export. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

How do I remove ads? Buy Countpeach Pro, from Remove ads under the banner or from Settings › Countpeach Pro. Ads disappear straight away.

I bought Pro on another device or reinstalled the app. Open Settings › Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Is Pro shared with my family? No. Family Sharing isn't available for Countpeach Pro.

Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads

Where do ads appear? In the free version, a small banner at the bottom of the counter list, History and Settings. Never on the counting screens or in widgets.

How do I report an inappropriate ad? Tap Report ad just above the banner, or Settings › Privacy & ads › Report an ad. This opens an About this ad sheet that shows whether the ad is personalised, why, and what data Google may use, with a button for your privacy choices. Tap Report this ad to email us with the screen, time and app version already filled in. Tell us what you saw if you can, and we'll block that kind of ad.

Privacy

Do you collect my counts? No. Counters and history stay on your device. There's no account and no analytics. Read the Privacy Policy.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? In the free version, in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, or a US state with a privacy law: Settings › Privacy & ads › Privacy choices (shown where a consent or opt-out message applies). For ad tracking on any device: iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, or Settings › Privacy & ads › Personalized ads in Countpeach.

Your data and backups

How do I move my counters to a new phone? Your counters are included in your normal iPhone or iPad backup. You can also use Settings › Back up & restore › Export backup to save a file (for example to iCloud Drive), then Import backup on the new device. Countpeach doesn't sync between devices yet.

Can I export to a spreadsheet? Yes, with Pro: History › Export CSV, or Settings › Export all as CSV.

I deleted a counter by mistake. Open Settings › Recently deleted. Deleted counters stay there for 30 days.

How do I delete all my data? Settings › Your data › Erase all counters and history, or delete the app. Backups and exported files are kept until you delete them.
