Countpeach Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026
App: Countpeach: Tally Counter (iPhone and iPad)
Who we are: Atul Das, trading as Peach & Rocket Studio. Address: Building 15, Street 986, Zone 22, Doha, Qatar. Phone: +974 3339 6945. Email: atuldasofficial@gmail.com. We make and run Countpeach. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which receives some data as described below.

The short version

  • Your counts stay on your device. Counters, names, targets, history and settings are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. We have no accounts, no servers for the app and no analytics, and we never receive your counts.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob on the counter list, History and Settings screens, never on the counting screens or in widgets. To serve and measure those ads, Google receives some device and usage data (listed below).
  • You choose about personalised ads. Ads are only personalised if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt and, where a consent message applies, agree to it. Otherwise ads are non-personalised. The app works the same whatever you choose.
  • Countpeach Pro removes ads. Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. With Pro, the app makes no ad requests.

1. What the app stores, and where

Countpeach saves your counters, counter names, colours, steps, targets, count history, settings and privacy choices only on your device, in the app's own storage. Whether you own Pro is checked on your device through Apple's App Store each time the app starts. We don't keep our own record of it. None of this is sent to us or to Google.

  • Widgets read and write the same on-device storage. Widget data doesn't leave your device.
  • Device backups: if your iPhone or iPad is backed up with iCloud Backup or to a computer, Countpeach's data is included in your backup, which Apple handles under your Apple settings and Apple's Privacy Policy. We can't access it.
  • Files you export: if you export a backup (Settings › Back up & restore › Export backup) or a CSV file, it goes where you choose (for example the Files app or iCloud Drive). It's then handled under your own settings and that service's privacy policy. We never receive it.
  • No sync in this version. Countpeach doesn't sync counters between devices. If we add iCloud sync later, we'll update this policy (and the App Store privacy label) before that version is released.
  • No analytics. We don't use any analytics, crash-reporting or attribution tools of our own.

2. Ads in the free version (Google AdMob)

The free version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), both provided by Google. Google is a separate company with its own privacy policy. According to Google's disclosure for app developers, the Mobile Ads SDK may collect and send to Google:

  • Coarse location, estimated from your IP address
  • Device ID: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking (section 4), or other app- or developer-scoped identifiers
  • Advertising data, such as which ads you've seen and whether you tapped them
  • Product interaction, such as app launches and taps on ads
  • Performance data, such as app launch time, hang rate or energy use
  • Crash data and other diagnostic data about the SDK

Google says it uses this kind of data for advertising and analytics: to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse and, if you allow it, to personalise ads. Google declares that crash, performance and other diagnostic data aren't linked to you or used for tracking. To check whether a consent message is needed and to remember your choices, the UMP SDK also contacts Google each time the free version starts. Google declares that this uses general location, performance and interaction data that isn't linked to you, only to run the consent message.

Read more in the Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and How Google uses cookies and other technologies in advertising. You can manage some Google ad settings at My Ad Center.

We don't receive this data in identifiable form. We only see aggregate AdMob reports, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned. We don't send Google your counts, counter names or history.

How we limit ads:

  • No ads on the counting screens, the grid view, any sheet, the Pro screen or widgets
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages (Google's "G" rating), with sensitive categories blocked
  • A Report ad option next to every banner (and Report an ad in Settings) opens an About this ad sheet in the app. It shows whether you're seeing personalised or non-personalised ads, why, and the kinds of data Google may use to choose them. It also links to your privacy choices (or the iOS tracking setting), and lets you report the ad by email (section 9)

3. Your privacy choices: consent messages

EEA, UK and Switzerland. If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Countpeach shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. On first launch, it appears before your first counter opens. It's provided by Google's User Messaging Platform, a consent tool certified under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. You can accept, refuse or choose purposes.

US states with privacy laws. If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (for example California), Google's US state privacy message lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising (section 11).

To change your choices at any time: open Settings › Privacy & ads › Privacy choices in Countpeach. This row appears in the free version wherever one of these messages applies. With Pro, the Privacy & ads section is hidden, because no ads are requested.

4. Tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency)

Countpeach never asks about tracking when you first open it. After you've used the app for a few days, we may show a short explanation once, followed by Apple's system prompt asking whether Countpeach may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. We only ask once.

  • If you choose Allow, Google may use your advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalise and measure ads.
  • If you choose Ask App Not to Track, or never answer, the app can't read your advertising identifier and ads aren't personalised.
  • You can change this any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking (or Settings › Countpeach). Once you've answered, the Settings › Privacy & ads › Personalized ads row in the free version takes you there. Apple explains this in If an app asks to track your activity.

When ads are personalised. Only when you've allowed tracking and, where a consent message applies, agreed to personalised ads and not opted out. In every other case ads are non-personalised. Google says these are based on context, such as the app and your general location, not on your past activity. Non-personalised ads still use limited data: your IP address and identifiers scoped to your device, which Google uses for frequency capping (limiting how often you see an ad), measurement and fraud prevention.

None of these choices changes what the app does. Every feature works the same whichever you choose.

5. Countpeach Pro and ads

Countpeach Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that removes all ads. With Pro:

  • no ads are shown and no ad requests are made
  • on every app launch where Pro is active, the app doesn't start the Google Mobile Ads SDK and doesn't run the consent check
  • if you buy Pro during a session in which the ad SDK has already started, ads stop immediately and no further ad requests are made, but the SDK stays loaded until you next close the app
  • on a new device, ads may appear until the app confirms your purchase with Apple (or you tap Restore purchase)
  • if a purchase is refunded or revoked, the free version's ads come back

6. In-app purchases

Countpeach Pro is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple tells the app on your device whether Pro is unlocked. We don't receive your name, email, payment details or Apple Account ID. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Information Apple may share with us

Apple gives developers aggregated App Store statistics, for example download counts and crash reports from people who chose to share them with developers. These statistics don't identify you, and we only use them to improve the app. You control this sharing in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

8. Children

Countpeach is a general-audience counting utility. It's not directed at children under 13 and isn't in the App Store Kids category. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads are limited to Google's G content rating.

  • If you're under the age at which you can consent to data use where you live (for example 13 in the UK, or 16 in Ireland), please don't agree to personalised ads or tracking. Ask a parent or guardian instead.
  • If you're a parent and believe your child has contacted us, email us and we'll delete what we hold. If a support email comes from a child, we reply once and then delete it, and we don't use it for anything else.

9. If you contact us or report an ad

If you email atuldasofficial@gmail.com, including from Contact or Report ad in the app, we receive your email address and whatever you write. Ad reports start from the About this ad sheet: tapping Report this ad opens an email to us with the screen, the time and the app version filled in, so we can find the ad, plus space to tell us what was wrong. Nothing is sent until you tap Send in your email app. We use this only to reply to you and to block unsuitable ads, and we never add you to a mailing list. Our email is hosted by Gmail (Google), which may store emails on servers outside your country, under the Google Privacy Policy. We delete support emails within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

10. Legal bases (UK GDPR and EU GDPR)

  • Personalised ads: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), given in the consent message and, for the advertising identifier, in Apple's tracking prompt. You can withdraw it at any time in Privacy choices or iOS Settings. Withdrawing doesn't affect processing that happened before.
  • Non-personalised ads (EEA, UK and Switzerland): storing or reading identifiers on your device needs your consent under ePrivacy rules (PECR in the UK), and Google asks publishers to get it. We collect that consent through the same message, and only request ads when the consent tool says ads may be requested. Where an app asks for your consent, Google says "we will use your data for the purposes described in the consent you give to the site or app" (How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services). Google's own privacy policy explains when Google relies on other bases, such as legitimate interests (for example fraud prevention and security).
  • Support emails and ad reports: our legitimate interest in answering you and keeping ads suitable (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Purchases: Apple processes payments as its own service, under Apple's terms.

11. US state privacy rights (including California)

We don't sell your personal information for money. However, if you allow personalised ads, the data Google collects through the ad SDK (identifiers, coarse location, and ad and app interactions) may count as a "sale" or "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) and similar state laws.

  • Opt out of sale or sharing: use Settings › Privacy & ads › Privacy choices where Google's US state message is shown, or turn off tracking in iOS Settings (section 4). Either way, ads become non-personalised.
  • Other rights: depending on your state, you can ask to know, access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you (normally only emails you've sent us), and we won't treat you differently for using these rights. Email us to make a request.
  • We don't collect sensitive personal information or precise location.

12. International transfers

Google may process ad data in countries outside your own, including the United States, under its own safeguards described in the Google Privacy Policy. We (in Qatar) don't receive this ad data.

13. Our website

14. Keeping and deleting your data

  • In the app: delete a counter (it stays in Recently deleted for 30 days, or remove it now with Delete now), reset a counter, or erase everything with Settings › Your data › Erase all counters and history.
  • Delete the app to remove all its data from that device. Copies may remain in your device backups and in any files you exported, until you delete them.
  • Support emails are deleted within 12 months (section 9).
  • Ad data is held by Google, not us. Use Privacy choices, the iOS Tracking setting and My Ad Center, or buy Pro to stop ad requests.

15. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because your counts never leave your device, most of this is in your hands in the app (section 14). For anything we hold, which is normally only emails you've sent us, email us and we'll help or point you to the right place. You can also complain to your data protection authority (for example the UK ICO or the Irish Data Protection Commission).

16. Changes to this policy

If we change how Countpeach handles data, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change ships, and update the app's App Store privacy label to match. We check the label against Google's SDK privacy manifest whenever we add or update the ad SDK. For significant changes, we'll also mention them in the App Store release notes.
